Output FPS: 25; capture requests 30 FPS; four buffers per camera.
Arrival times use the host monotonic clock immediately after OpenCV read().
They are not sensor exposure timestamps and do not establish synchronization.
Source sequences count successful reads; repeats flag reuse of the same read.
Identical exposures returned as separate reads cannot be detected this way.
Late output slots repeat the latest frames and are flagged in the CSV.
Segments identify recording resumes; pauses are omitted from AVI time.
